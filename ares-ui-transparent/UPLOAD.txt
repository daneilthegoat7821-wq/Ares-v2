ARES UI / TRANSPARENT PNG ASSETS

Upload the 19 PNG files into one folder named ares-v2 in Heavens-assets.
Keep filenames exactly as supplied. Do not upload a ZIP as a substitute for PNGs.
The image loader will use the raw PNG URLs after upload.

All PNGs have actual RGBA transparency. The dark contact-sheet background is
only a preview and is not part of these PNG files. Open interior spaces and
the background around the character are transparent. Glow strength is baked
into partial alpha, so the runtime may dim it further with draw.Image alpha.
